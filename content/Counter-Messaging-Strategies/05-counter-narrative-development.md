## Purpose

You'll learn when and how to develop counter-narratives—understanding that narratives address needs that facts alone don't reach.

---

## Scenario

Debunking specific claims isn't working. The underlying narrative persists even when individual claims are corrected. You need a different approach. You have:

- **Time available**: Strategic timeframe (months, not days)
- **Stakes**: Competing for hearts and minds, not just accuracy
- **Data access**: Audience research, narrative analysis
- **Constraint**: Counter-narratives are hard to engineer authentically

---

## Task

Produce three outputs:

1. **Assessment**: Is counter-narrative the right approach here?
2. **Confidence level**: Low / Medium / High
3. **Next action**: Counter-narrative strategy or alternative approach

---

## Method: Counter-Narrative Development Workflow

### Start here: Understand why narrative persists

Before developing counter-narrative, ask:
- What need does this narrative serve? (Identity, community, meaning, agency)
- Why do fact-based corrections fail?
- What's the underlying story structure?

**If narrative serves deep needs, debunking claims won't dislodge it.**

### Step 2: Assess counter-narrative feasibility

Counter-narratives work best when:
- Alternative narrative can serve same needs
- Credible messengers exist within the community
- You have time for narrative to develop organically
- You're not trying to engineer from outside

Counter-narratives struggle when:
- No authentic alternative exists
- Outside actors are creating it (authenticity problem)
- Short timeline requires faster interventions

### Step 3: Design with authenticity constraints

**Don't**: Create fake grassroots counter-narratives
**Do**: Amplify existing authentic alternatives
**Don't**: Preach at target audience
**Do**: Support voices from within community

## Narrative vs Claim

**Claims** are specific assertions:
- "Vaccine X causes Y" (can be fact-checked)
- "Election fraud occurred in Z" (can be investigated)

**Narratives** are story structures:
- "Elites are deceiving ordinary people"
- "Our way of life is under attack"
- "They're coming for our children"

**Debunking addresses claims. Counter-narratives address stories.**

When you debunk a claim but the narrative persists, new claims emerge to fit the story.

---

## Counter-Narrative Elements

**Alternative explanation**: Different story explaining same observations
**Need satisfaction**: Addresses same psychological/social needs
**In-group messenger**: Credible voice from within the community
**Emotional resonance**: Compelling, not just accurate
**Authenticity**: Emerges from community, not imposed

**Warning**: Manufactured counter-narratives often fail. Authentic alternatives, amplified, work better.

---

## Evidence Ladder

**Strong signals** (counter-narrative appropriate):
- Underlying narrative identified clearly
- Needs narrative serves are understood
- Authentic alternative voices exist
- Long-term engagement possible

**Medium signals** (challenging but possible):
- Narrative understood but needs unclear
- Few existing alternative voices
- Medium-term timeline

**Weak signals** (counter-narrative may not work):
- Trying to engineer narrative from outside
- No authentic messengers available
- Short timeline
- Claims are the problem, not narrative

---

## Stop Rule

Stop counter-narrative development when:
- **Authenticity impossible**: No genuine alternative exists
- **Outside imposition**: You're creating, not amplifying
- **Wrong diagnosis**: Problem is claims, not narrative
- **Resources better spent**: Other interventions more effective

---

## DIM Mapping

Counter-narratives operate at Gen 3-5 level:

**Gen 3** (Prebunking): Inoculate against narrative structures
**Gen 5** (Interaction): Create conditions where healthy narratives flourish

**Not Gen 2**: Counter-narratives aren't debunking at narrative scale—they're offering alternatives.

**Key insight**: You can't debunk someone's identity story. You can offer a different story that serves their needs better.

---

## Key Takeaway

Counter-narratives address what debunking can't: the underlying stories that persist even when individual claims fail. They must be authentic, serve the needs the harmful narrative serves, and come from credible voices within communities. You can't engineer them from outside—but you can support and amplify authentic alternatives.
